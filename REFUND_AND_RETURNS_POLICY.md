# Refund & Returns Policy

## Overview

At NutriBox Trading W.L.L, we source authentic health and wellness products from multiple international and regional brands to serve customers across Qatar. If you are not happy with a product, you may be eligible for a refund, exchange, or store credit under the conditions listed below.

## Eligibility

- Items must be returned within 14 days of delivery.
- Products must be unopened, unused, and in their original packaging.
- For hygiene reasons, supplements and personal care items cannot be returned once opened.
- Refunds are not available for products marked as final sale unless there is a manufacturing defect.

## How to Request a Return

1. Contact our support team through the website or your account dashboard.
2. Provide your order number, product name, and reason for return.
3. Our support team will review your request and provide return instructions.

## Return Shipping

- Customers are responsible for return shipping unless the return is due to our error or a defective product.
- We recommend using a tracked shipping method so the package can be monitored during transit.

## Refund Process

- Once your returned item is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.
- Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method within 7 business days.
- If you used a gift card or store credit, the refund will be returned in the same form.

## Exchanges

- If you want to exchange a product, contact customer support so we can help you select the correct replacement.
- Exchanges are subject to product availability.

## Defective or Damaged Items

- If your product arrives damaged or defective, please report it immediately.
- We will arrange a replacement or refund once the issue is verified.

## Exceptions

- Some products, especially supplements and consumables, may have special return rules. These will be clearly stated on the product page.
- Promotional items and bundled offers may also have modified return terms.

## Contact

If you have questions about a return or refund, please reach out to our support team via the website contact form or support email.
