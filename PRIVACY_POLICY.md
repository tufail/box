# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

NutriBox Trading W.L.L is a Qatar-based retailer of multi-brand health and wellness products. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and how we keep it safe.

## Information We Collect

- Contact information such as name, email address, phone number, and shipping address.
- Order and payment details necessary to process purchases.
- Browser and device information to improve site performance and security.
- Optional profile information you provide when creating an account.

## How We Use Your Information

- To process and fulfill orders for products supplied from multiple brands.
- To communicate order status, shipping updates, and support inquiries.
- To improve our product selection, services, and website experience.
- To send marketing and product update information where permitted.

## Subscription Emails

- If you subscribe, we may send you marketing updates and product announcements.
- Subscription email addresses are intended only for our internal use for marketing, product updates, and Qatar-specific promotions.
- We do not share subscription email addresses with third parties for external marketing without your explicit consent.
- You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in our emails or by contacting support.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

- We may share personal data with service providers who help us operate the website and fulfill orders.
- We do not sell your personal information.
- We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

## Security

- We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information.
- Although we strive to protect your data, no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Cookies and Tracking

- We use cookies to manage your session, personalize content, and analyze site usage.
- You can manage cookie settings through your browser preferences.

## Your Rights

- You can request access to the personal information we hold about you.
- You can request corrections to inaccurate information.
- You can request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal or contractual obligations.

## Changes to This Policy

- We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
- Changes will be posted on the site with a revised effective date.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our customer support team.
